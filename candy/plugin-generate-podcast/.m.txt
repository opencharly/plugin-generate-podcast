feat(emit): the episode emitter, and a schema fix that restored its type safety

The emitter turns a VALIDATED #Episode into the episode markdown and the cue lines podcast-render
consumes, so the script a listener reads and the audio they hear are the same content in two
substrates -- and a malformed episode cannot reach the synthesiser because it cannot be constructed.

It consumes the GENERATED types (params.Episode/params.Segment) rather than a hand-written shape,
which is also the proof those generated types are usable.

The cast is ROLES, not names: Cast{Enthusiast, Anchor} are the caller's, and the emitter refuses a
role that is not in the episode's cast. Nothing here hardcodes a host name -- the news desk's own
pair (CONCH/TEMPER) is one profile's choice.

Two guards cover what the generated type cannot express: both cast roles must be named and present
in the cast, and the episode must have at least one segment. The required fields, the beats and the
3..5 count stay the SCHEMA's job -- re-checking them in Go would be a hand-transcribed second copy.

SCHEMA FIX, found by compiling against it: `segments!: [...#Segment] & [_, _, _]` satisfies the count
but DEGRADES the generated element type to `[]any` (the generated file read
`Segments []any/* CUE closed list */`), silently losing #Segment on the Go side. Length constraints
now live in hidden fields -- `_min: len(segments) >= 3` / `_max: len(segments) <= 5` -- and the
regenerated type is `Segments []Segment`. The `!` was deliberately dropped from those hidden fields:
a required hidden field would demand the author supply it.
